03 / REVIEW YOUR ROUTES

Your plan, ready to explore

Solana slices request live indicative USDC quotes from Jupiter. Open a Solana swap here using Jupiter, or continue at the venue. Each slice requires a separate wallet approval. Opening a swap does not execute it. Issuer and venue eligibility still apply.

Built on traceable data

Verified identities

Token addresses come from the public xStocks API and Ondo's official token list. The catalog covers the networks those sources expose, not every issuer or deployment in existence. Type labels are inferred from product names.

Observed markets

DEX Screener supplies prices, 24-hour changes, volume and liquidity for the highest-liquidity indexed pool where the verified token is the base asset. Retrieval times are displayed; recent retrieval does not guarantee recent trades. A missing pool does not prove a token is untradeable elsewhere.

History & quotes

GeckoTerminal supplies actual daily pool closes. Jupiter supplies indicative Solana quotes at your selected USDC size. Neither is a traditional stock-exchange feed. No invented historical returns or issuer safety scores are used.

Your wallet & plans

Connecting an installed Solana wallet reveals its public address only. The embedded Jupiter swap requests approval only when you choose to trade. Plans and watchlists stay in this browser. Each slice is purchased separately; automatic DCA and cross-chain basket execution are not enabled.
