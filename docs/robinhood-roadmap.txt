Folio: Robinhood Chain roadmap (deferred)
Saved: 2026-09-26

Product direction
Keep one discovery page for stocks and ETFs. Group comparable exposures by underlying, but preserve issuer, chain, contract address, eligibility and product rights for every version. Robinhood Chain is a separate Ethereum-compatible L2, not Ethereum mainnet.

Phases
1. Stabilize the Solana buy, receive, holdings and sell experience.
2. Add Robinhood Stock Tokens to discovery using the official registry and API. Respect approved terminology and regional restrictions.
3. Add an EVM wallet connection and a separately validated trading adapter for a supported venue on Robinhood Chain. Check actual liquidity and supported payment tokens; do not assume Solana USDC is usable there.
4. Only later consider optional cross-chain funding with explicit fees, timing, destination asset and recovery states. Never imply that two chain transactions are atomic.

Balances and custody
Keep funds in user wallets. Show balances separately by chain and contract. An aggregate USD value is informational, not a pooled spendable balance. Robinhood Chain uses ETH for gas. Each chain requires its own route, signature and confirmation tracking.

Integration gates
Confirm eligible countries, issuer restrictions, API permissions, exact token addresses, token multipliers, live routes and sale support. A matching ticker does not establish fungibility or equivalent investor rights. Review the business model and fee collection before public trading access.
